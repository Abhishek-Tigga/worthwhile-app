Support

Help with Worthwhile, and how to reach us.

Write to us

Something not working, or an idea for an island? Tap Feedback at the top of Home in the app, or email us. We usually reply within 2 to 4 working days.

Email support@tryworthwhile.app

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Does leaving the app pause my session?

No. Your session keeps going while you use other apps or lock your phone. When you come back, the island catches up.

Will I lose my islands if I change phones?

Not if backup is on. Install Worthwhile again with the same Apple Account, on this iPhone or a new one, and your history comes back.

How do I stop sharing how I use the app?

Settings, Privacy, Share usage data. Nothing more is sent after that.

How do I turn backup off?

Settings, Privacy, Back up my progress. New history then stays only on this phone. What was already backed up stays on our server until you delete your data.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, Data, Delete my data. You need to be online. This removes your backup, feedback, ratings and usage data from our services and clears the app on your phone. Deleting the app alone does not remove the backup. You can also email us. See the privacy policy.

Who can use Worthwhile?

People aged 18 and over.
